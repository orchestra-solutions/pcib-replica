PCI Booking is now ISO 27001 certified

ISO 27001

PCI Booking is delighted to announce that we have obtained ISO 27001:2022 certification; the internationally recognised top-tier certification for information security management. Developed by the world’s best experts in the field of information security, ISO (The International Standards Organisation) 27001:2022 is considered the benchmark for information security. The certification applies to our entire suite of products and features.

While PCI Booking has been PCI DSS Level 1 PCI Compliant since 2012, we pride ourselves in guaranteeing the highest level of confidentiality, integrity, and data security. For that reason we have taken the extra step of obtaining ISO 27001:2022.

On route of achieving such certification, PCI Booking was required to undergo stringent and repeated assessment in order to demonstrate our commitment to continually achieving the highest standards of data security control. ISO 27001:2022 certification reassures our customers that their trust in our services is well founded and the security of their data is in safe hands with PCI Booking.
