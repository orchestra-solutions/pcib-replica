Data Tokenization

PCI Booking’s Data tokenization uses the technology developed to keep payment information secure and adopts it for use across all data files. Ransomware attacks demonstrate the havoc that losing access to your customers data, even if it’s not payment information, can wreak.

Understanding the Power of Tokenization

In the digital age, the specter of cyberattacks looms large, making the protection of sensitive data an imperative for businesses. Cyberthreats, especially ransomware attacks, exploit vulnerabilities in data security, causing significant disruptions. PCI Booking’s secure vault which tokenizes and shields payment information, also extends and offers robust security protocols to all your data files as well. The recent surge in ransomware incidents showcases the chaos that the compromise of customer data, even beyond payment information, can wreak.

The Benefits of Data Tokenization

As cyberthreats continue to evolve, so should our defense mechanisms. Data Tokenization stands as a formidable barrier against cybercriminal tactics. Through tokenization, sensitive data is replaced with encrypted tokens, rendering it meaningless to unauthorized entities. In the unfortunate event of a breach, cybercriminals encounter only meaningless tokens instead of the original sensitive information. This method not only secures sensitive data but also mitigates the impact of potential breaches.
