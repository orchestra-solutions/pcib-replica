DIGITAL WALLETS

Powering Digital Wallet Payments

Now offering integration for Google Pay and PayPal digital wallet payments

Take advantage of PCI Booking’s single API endpoint to integrate with not only over 70 different payment gateways, but also multiple different digital wallet payment methods. Currently PCI Booking supports Google Pay and PayPal digital wallets. Start accepting payments from digital wallets today.

The rapid rise in popularity of digital wallets has come from the ease-of-use and added protections that they offer. PCI Booking is currently working on adding additional digital wallets to our offering.

Single integration to all digital wallets

Process payments through your choice of digital wallet

Don’t see your preferred choice? Add additional digital wallets for free
