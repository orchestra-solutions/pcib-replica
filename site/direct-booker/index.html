DIRECT BOOKER x PCI BOOKING

Process payments right from your PMS

Successful hotels need to have the ability to process payments quickly and easily. Through Direct Booker’s partnership with PCI Booking, you can now process payments directly from your PMS to your choice of payment processor.

PCI Booking supports dozens of different payment processors and you are sure to find the one you need. In case you do not see the one you would like to use, contact us with their details and we will endeavor to add it to the list.

The integration work has already been completed and you can start processing payments today!

What will you need?

  • An account with the payment processor that you wish to use.
  • An account with PCI Booking.
  • That’s it, you’re good to go!
