PCI Booking will be discussing PCI compliance options at ITB Berlin

  • Post published:February 22, 2019
  • Post category:Events

Join us at ITB Berlin (Hall 5.1 / 121) where our PCI compliance experts will be discussing how travel companies can protect their customers credit card information and become PCI compliant.

What we will be discussing:

  1. PCI Compliant Card Capture

Regardless of how you receive credit card data, whether it’s from your website to your API, pulled from a third party or requested from the card holder, you need to make sure that the card information is sent securely and is handled as per the PCI compliance guidelines. PCI Booking can offer you a simple to implement “PCI compliant shield” around your business to capture the card or virtual card before it reaches your system infrastructure and keeps the data secure in a PCI DSS Level 1 environment.

  1. Storage: Safe and Secure

As data breaches become ever more frequent and unprecedented in nature, it is vital for all businesses capturing traveler credit card details to store them in a way that can be both accessible to the business but protected and inaccessible to others. Check out our unlimited secure storage and management tools to keep the cards up-to-date.

  1. Compliant Transmission and Charge

Once you have captured the card details, you will then need to use them. This could mean sending to a third party, such as a hotel, car rental or restaurant. Alternatively, it could be that you need to charge the card directly for your fees. Whatever your requirements, PCI Booking has the tools in place to do so securely.

Our specialist team look forward to welcoming you to our booth to further discuss your strategic PCI requirements.

Simply visit our stand at Hall 5.1 / 121 and drop of your business card to be in with a chance of winning!
