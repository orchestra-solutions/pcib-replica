PAYMENTS LIBRARY

Seamless Payment Collection with Full Flexibility

Accept payments effortlessly with PCI Booking’s Payments Library, a lightweight JavaScript integration that lets you collect, tokenize, and charge payments directly from your website—all while maintaining full PCI compliance.

Why Choose Payments Library?

Multiple Payment Options

Support credit & debit card payment, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal—choose which methods to display for each transaction.

Flexible Payment Actions

Decide per transaction whether to tokenize, charge, or tokenize & charge—all from the same integration.

Payment Processor Agnostic

Process the payment through any of our integrated PSPs based on your needs.

Simple & Fast Integration

Integrate with just a few lines of JavaScript and start accepting payments in minutes.

Full PCI Compliance

Reduce compliance burden with our secure tokenization—no sensitive data touches your servers.

Customizable User Experience

Seamlessly embed payment buttons that match your brand and provide a smooth checkout experience.

How It Works

1.

Integrate in Minutes

Add the Payments Library JavaScript snippet to your website and configure your settings.

2.

Choose Payment Methods

Enable Cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or PayPal based on your business needs.

3.

Define the Transaction Type

Decide whether to tokenize, charge, or tokenize & charge—with full control per transaction.

4.

Collect Payments Securely

Payments are processed via PCI Booking’s secure environment, keeping you out of PCI scope.

Token Compatibility

Payment methods tokenized by the Payments Library are the same tokens generated in any other method in PCI Booking (card form, push and pull tokenization, tokenization by link, etc). Customers can keep their existing token handling processes as they are with the Payments Library as another source to tokenization.

Built for Businesses of All Sizes

Whether you’re a SaaS provider, travel company, marketplace, or e-commerce platform, Payments Library offers a scalable and secure way to accept and process payments.

Read our documentation and start accepting payments today!
