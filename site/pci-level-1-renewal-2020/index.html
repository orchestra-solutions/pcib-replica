PCI Booking announces renewal of PCI DSS Level 1 Certification

  • Post published:December 17, 2019
  • Post category:Product News / Shield

PCI Booking is happy to announce that we have renewed our PCI DSS Level 1 PCI Compliance for 2020. This latest Level 1 certification continues on a mark first achieved in 2012, when PCI Booking became fully compliant with this highest level of PCI accreditation.

PCI DSS is a set of security standards designed to ensure that merchants, regardless of size, who receive, store, process or transmit payment card information maintain strict information security controls and processes.

As part of the accreditation process, PCI Booking was required to demonstrate to independent auditors that we are not only keeping current with the latest PCI-DSS standards and requirements, but are also constantly ensuring that our system meets the highest security protocols set by PCI DSS.

For customers of PCI Booking, once again renewing this PCI DSS Level 1 accreditation highlights our commitment to protecting the personal data entrusted with PCI Booking.
