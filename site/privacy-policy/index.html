PCI Booking Privacy Policy

PRIVACY POLICY

Effective May 13, 2019


PCI Booking recognizes the importance of protecting the privacy of all information provided by its users through the PCI Booking website, as well as through the use of its products and services. PCI Booking has created the following policy guidelines in order to ensure that its users’ rights to privacy are fully respected.

What types of information does PCI Booking gather?

Personal information is collected through various registration and request forms. The forms require the user to insert contact information such as name and email address. Unique identifiers such as a password and user name are collected to verify your identity, as well as to identify your account in our record system.

Contact information is used to send our users billing and account information, and updates on the company and its services. Upon request, PCI Booking will enable users to “opt-out” of any further service notifications.

PCI Booking acquires its users’ IP addresses, in order to help diagnose problems with its server.

Personal and Contact Information

PCI Booking will not reveal any personal information that users provide to us to a third party without permission. However, if PCI Booking has determined a user to have abused the service, i.e. used the service contrary to the Terms of Service, we reserve the right to disclose the identity of users to any party, including but not limited to law enforcement bodies, to stop abuse of the service.

Information in Fax Messages

Information sent through the XML and fax messaging network by registered clients will be confidential. Specific information will be accessed only in cases where support or repair is required. In such cases, information from messages sent through the PCI Booking system will not be disclosed to anyone other than those required to repair the problem. Additionally, sample messages may be monitored by PCI Booking for quality assurance purposes.

Information sent through the fax and XML messaging network by users of our free sending service will be monitored for compliance with the free service usage guidelines.

External Links

Our site contains links to external sites. PCI Booking is not responsible for the information or data collection processess of these external sites; the information is simply provided as a service to our customers. If you have questions about the data collection practices of linked sites, please contact those companies directly.

Cookies

During the course of your visit to this site, cookies may be transferred to your browser. If you do not wish to accept cookies from this site, please follow the instructions at AboutCookies.org. Note that this may cause parts of this site to become unusable.

Questions

If you have any questions about this privacy statement, the practices of this site, or your transactions through PCI Booking, you can contact PCI Booking by submitting website feedback.
